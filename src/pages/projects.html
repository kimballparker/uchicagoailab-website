LeaseChat

Renters often sign leases they don't fully understand, and finding out what the law says means sorting through both the lease and the rules of their city and state. LeaseChat does that work for them, for free.

  • Analyzes a renter's lease in plain English
  • Explains the law in the renter's city and state
  • Helps draft letters to a landlord

Case study

How LeaseChat came together.

The problem

Landlord-tenant law varies from state to state and city to city. A renter trying to understand their rights has to read their lease and then figure out what their local law says about it. Most don't have a lawyer to help.

The first plan

The team started by building a database of carefully researched summaries of rental law across the country, planning to put AI on top of it for better accuracy than a general chatbot.

The pivot

Frontier models improved quickly during the quarter. The team tested the latest models against their research and decided to put their effort into prompt engineering and careful product design instead. That decision is a big part of why they shipped on time.

The launch

LeaseChat launched free to the public in December 2025. Students handled everything from product design to press outreach, and the launch was covered by legal trade press and local TV.
