Our mission

Too many people face legal problems without a lawyer. We believe the next generation of lawyers should build the tools that close that gap, not just use them.

01

Access to justice

We pick problems where people need legal help but can't afford it. We build tools and drive adoption at scale.

02

Builders, not just users

Students learn product, research, AI design, legal risk, and distribution, the full work of a legal-tech founder.

03

Shipped, not theoretical

Every cohort ends with a real product in the hands of real people, not a paper or a prototype.

How the Lab works

One quarter. One product. Run like a startup.

Students work as a founding team. They learn what it takes to bring a legal product to market, from the first user interview to the press launch.

Find the gap

Pick a legal problem millions of people face without help, and define who we're building for.

Master the law

Research the law across jurisdictions and decide where the product must be precise and careful.

Build with AI

Design prompts, workflows, and guardrails with frontier models, then test until the product works.

Ship it

Launch to the public, pitch the press, and get the product into the hands of people who need it.
