About the Lab

From technology users
to technology builders.

The AI Lab is an immersive seminar at the University of Chicago Law School. Students use AI to take on pressing legal problems, from expanding access to justice to simplifying compliance, and leave ready to lead where law and technology meet.

Why we exist

The law should work for everyone.

Most people who face a legal problem, like a confusing lease, a workplace dispute, or a government form, never talk to a lawyer. The cost is too high and the process is too opaque. Generative AI makes it possible to change that, but only if the people building these tools understand the law deeply and take its risks seriously.

That's where law students come in. The AI Lab gives them a hands-on way to build: in a single quarter, a team of students identifies a real legal need, researches the law behind it, designs an AI product, and launches it to the public.

Along the way they learn what no casebook can teach. They learn how to scope a product, work with frontier models, manage legal risk, write for real users, and take something from idea to launch.

What we value

How we work.

Ship over theorize

A tool in people's hands teaches more than a perfect plan. We set deadlines and meet them.

Rigor where it counts

Legal information has to be right. We test hard, cite carefully, and are clear about what our tools do and don't do.

Adapt as AI moves

Models improve month to month. We stay flexible and change our approach when the technology changes.

Built for reach

We build for people who can't afford a lawyer, and we measure success by how many of them we reach.

Every skill counts

Our students come from engineering, PR, policy, and more. A strong team uses all of it.

Information, not advice

Our tools help people understand the law. They don't replace a lawyer when one is needed.

Our story

The Lab so far.

  1. August 2025

    The AI Lab is announced

    UChicago Law introduces a first-of-its-kind seminar in which students will build a legal-tech tool for renters nationwide.

  2. Autumn 2025

    The inaugural cohort

    Ten students begin building. As frontier models improve during the quarter, the team shifts its approach to stay ahead.

  3. December 2025

    LeaseChat launches

    A free AI tool that helps renters understand their leases and rights goes live, with coverage from the legal and local press.

  4. Now

    The next build

    A new cohort is at work on the Lab's next product.
