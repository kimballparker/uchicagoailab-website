Law360 Pulse reporter Matt Perez covered the launch of LeaseChat, the free AI tool AI Lab students designed to help renters understand their leases and legal rights.

The story traces LeaseChat back to the Lab's announcement in August, when students were given a mandate to build a chatbot that could help renters nationwide. It also quotes Kimball Dean Parker on why the tool helps renters understand both the law and their own lease.
