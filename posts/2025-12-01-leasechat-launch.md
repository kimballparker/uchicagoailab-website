**CHICAGO (December 2, 2025) –** The AI Lab at the [University of Chicago Law School](https://www.law.uchicago.edu/) today launched [LeaseChat](https://www.getleasechat.com/), a free AI tool that helps renters across the country analyze their lease and understand their legal rights. With more than 40 million rented properties in the United States, the goal of LeaseChat is to provide new tools to support as many of those renters as possible.

LeaseChat helps renters in several ways:

- **Lease Analyzer:** Renters can upload their lease, and LeaseChat will analyze the terms and point out any red flags.
- **Lease Chat:** Renters can ask questions about their lease, and LeaseChat will respond in plain language with citations to the lease terms.
- **Legal Rights:** LeaseChat takes the location of the lease and outlines the renter's rights and applicable laws in that city and state, including timing for repairs, notice requirements for landlord entry, deposit return rules, and more.
- **Letter Drafter:** Renters can use LeaseChat to draft letters to their landlords about repairs, late rent, deposit returns, and more.

"It's difficult for renters to know their legal rights because they need to understand both the law and their lease," said Kimball Dean Parker, head of the AI Lab at the University of Chicago Law School. "LeaseChat helps with both. Our hope is that this tool can level the playing field and help millions of renters to know and exercise their rights."

The AI Lab was founded in 2025 at the University of Chicago Law School with the explicit goal of building AI tools that help people who can't afford or otherwise access attorneys. There are 10 law students who participated in the LeaseChat project, building the tool from scratch during the fall term. Students researched the law, coded the platform, designed the user experience, and conducted the outreach.

"The AI Lab has helped open my eyes to what the latest technology can do and what it's like to build an AI product for a population that needs help," said Nanako Watanabe, a law student participating in the AI Lab. "It's one of the most meaningful experiences I've had in law school."

"The AI Lab provides a unique learning experience for our students to build and launch an AI product," said Adam Chilton, dean of the University of Chicago Law School and the Howard G. Krane Professor of Law. "It also produces a tremendous public benefit that can help millions of people across the nation."

To learn more about LeaseChat, please visit [www.getleasechat.com](https://www.getleasechat.com).
