In August 2025, the University of Chicago Law School announced the AI Lab: a new seminar in which law students would build a generative AI tool and release it to the public to help people understand and navigate the law.

The story, by Nadia Alfadel Coloma, explains where the idea came from. The Law School's AI committee, chaired by Deputy Dean William H. J. Hubbard, had spent two years tracking how AI is changing the legal profession and finding ways to bring it into the curriculum. The AI Lab grew out of that work.
