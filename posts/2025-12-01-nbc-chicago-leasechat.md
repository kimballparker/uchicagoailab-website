summary: NBC 5 Responds visited the AI Lab to see how law students built LeaseChat, a free AI tool that explains a renter's lease and the laws where they live.
---

NBC 5 Responds reporter Kate Chappell visited the AI Lab, where ten law students and their professor spent the Lab's first term on renters' rights.

The story walks through how LeaseChat works: a renter uploads a lease, and the tool analyzes what's in it, along with the laws in the renter's city and state, almost instantly. Kimball Dean Parker explains the goal of helping as many renters across the country as possible.
