UChicago News takes a look inside the AI Lab's first term, when ten law students built and launched [LeaseChat](https://www.getleasechat.com), a free AI tool that helps renters understand their leases and their rights.

The story, by Nadia Alfadel Coloma, follows the decisions the team made along the way. They put their effort into prompt engineering instead of building a custom legal database, and they shifted from partner outreach to pitching the media, which earned local news coverage. It also covers what happened after launch: LeaseChat has helped hundreds of people, and the Lab is working with local governments to refer renters to the tool.
