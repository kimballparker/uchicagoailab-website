On LawSites, legal technology journalist Bob Ambrogi reported on LeaseChat's launch, noting that the Lab's ten inaugural students shipped their first product less than four months after the Law School announced the AI Lab.

The piece walks through LeaseChat's core features for renters and points out that every feature is also available in Spanish.
