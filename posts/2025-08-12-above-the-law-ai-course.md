Above the Law's Chris Williams covered the announcement of the AI Lab, a new UChicago Law course in which students would build an AI tool to help the public understand renters' rights.

The story highlights the course's hands-on approach: students would research the law, take an entrepreneurial approach to building a legal tech product, and interview people about the questions they have to make sure the tool is useful.
